AI Classification
5 FACE SHAPES

Our EfficientNet model classifies your facial geometry into one of five categories — each with its own curated set of recommended and avoided styles.

HEART

Wider forehead and cheekbones tapering to a pointed chin.

Soften wide forehead; keep top balanced
OBLONG

Longer than wide with fairly uniform width from forehead to jaw.

Reduce face length; avoid excess height
OVAL

Balanced proportions with slightly wider cheekbones — the "ideal" shape.

Most styles work — avoid extremes
ROUND

Similar width and length with soft, curved features.

Add height and angles to reduce roundness
SQUARE

Strong angular jawline with forehead approximately equal to jaw width.

Texture and clean structure work best

Under the Hood
HOW IT
WORKS
01
Upload & Detect

MediaPipe locates your face in the photo, applies eye-landmark rotation correction, and crops with smart margins.

⬡ MediaPipe · OpenCV
02
Classify Shape

A fine-tuned EfficientNetB0 CNN (260×260 px) runs inference across 5 face shape classes using a softmax layer.

⬡ TensorFlow · EfficientNetB0
03
Blend Results

Top-2 predicted shapes are blended using confidence-weighted scoring for nuanced, hybrid recommendations.

⬡ Python · Confidence Weighting
04
Pull from DB

Recommendations and avoids are fetched from 8-table PostgreSQL schema — personalised, fast, and fully relational.

⬡ PostgreSQL 16 · psycopg2
